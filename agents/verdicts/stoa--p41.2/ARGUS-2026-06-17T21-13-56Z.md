## RULING -- the F1 unarmed-consumer question (the judgment the floor-manager asked ARGUS to make)

VERDICT ON THE QUESTION: the CLAUDE.md line IS an acceptable RELIABLE carrier for the hooks-unarmed consumer case; the unarmed-consumer state is NOT a residual gap that blocks. Reasoning:

1. The directive (Verified-premise, P-FALLBACK clause) names the reliable carriers explicitly: a RELIABLE carrier reads the signal file (the Stop self-check AND/OR CLAUDE.md, the substrate proven channels), and surfacing must never depend on additionalContext ALONE. CLAUDE.md is named by the directive ITSELF as one of the two proven channels, co-equal with the Stop self-check via and/or. So a CLAUDE.md reader is, by the directive own standard, a qualifying reliable carrier -- not a degraded fallback.
2. The load-bearing bar the directive sets is precise and NEGATIVE: never additionalContext ALONE. rev2 meets it in BOTH arming states. Armed: the Stop-hook reason reader (section 2.8) is the strongest carrier, structurally off-additionalContext (the script header L12-15 confirms Stop supports decision/reason and NOT additionalContext -- a genuinely independent channel; verified in stop-self-check.sh). Unarmed: the CLAUDE.md line is read by the agent regardless of hooks. In neither state does surfacing collapse onto additionalContext-alone. The directive bar is therefore met everywhere.
3. The honest residual (name it, do not inflate it): the two carriers are NOT equally strong. The Stop-hook reader is mechanical (fires deterministically when armed); the CLAUDE.md line is an instruction the agent must choose to honor (the same soft-compliance surface every CLAUDE.md instruction has). So on an unarmed consumer the F1 guarantee is at its WEAKER (instruction-grade, not hook-grade) carrier. That is a real strength-asymmetry worth recording -- but it does NOT violate the directive never-additionalContext-alone bar, because the CLAUDE.md instruction is still a non-additionalContext channel. The design names this asymmetry honestly (section 5 weak-point 3). I concur with its framing.

RULING IN ONE LINE: acceptable -- CLAUDE.md is a directive-named reliable carrier and the never-additionalContext-alone bar holds in both armed and unarmed states; the only residual is the (honestly-disclosed, non-blocking) fact that the unarmed carrier is instruction-grade not hook-grade.
