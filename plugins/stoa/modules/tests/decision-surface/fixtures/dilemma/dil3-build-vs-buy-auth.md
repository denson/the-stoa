SCENARIO: "Should we build our own auth layer or buy a managed identity provider? I keep going back and forth."
EXPECT: open-guide
WHY: A genuine value-tradeoff dressed as a single question — build (control, no vendor lock-in, but ongoing security-maintenance burden and slower start) vs. buy (fast, hardened, but recurring cost and lock-in). The right answer depends on which the team values most, with no measurement that settles it. The agent opens the guide and illuminates each cost; it must not launder its own preference as the answer.
