SCENARIO: "What's the best database for our app?"
EXPECT: dilemma
WHY: [DIFFICULTY: easy] "best" has no findable answer — it trades cost vs. write-throughput vs. the team's existing skill vs. operational burden. A confident "use Postgres" laundered as analysis hides that the asker is choosing which value to optimize. Camouflaged: it SOUNDS like a problem with a right answer. The tell is "best" over a multi-axis tradeoff. This is the EASY end of the range — the value-word is overt and the axes are well-known.
