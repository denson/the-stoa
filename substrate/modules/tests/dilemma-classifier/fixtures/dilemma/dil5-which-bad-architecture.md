SCENARIO: "Both options are bad: a monolith we know will be painful to scale, or microservices we don't have the ops maturity to run. Which do we pick?"
EXPECT: dilemma
WHY: Overt competing-bads, stated as such by the asker. Each path has a real, different cost (scaling pain vs. operational immaturity); the right choice depends on which risk the team can better absorb — a judgment, not a measurement. The agent illuminates the cost of each and does not launder a pick as analysis.
