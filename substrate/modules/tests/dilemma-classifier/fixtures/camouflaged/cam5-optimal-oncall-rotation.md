SCENARIO: "What's the optimal on-call rotation length for our team — one week or two?"
EXPECT: dilemma
WHY: [DIFFICULTY: hard] Hard because it looks like an operations question with best-practice literature behind it ("optimal" + a bounded two-option choice invites a confident cited answer). But the axes genuinely conflict and weight differently per team: one-week (less burnout per shift, more context-switching/handoff overhead, more frequent disruption) vs. two-week (deeper context, fewer handoffs, heavier burnout load on the person holding it). "Optimal" depends on whether you optimize for burnout, continuity, or fairness — a values call the team owns. The camouflage is the appeal to a supposed best-practice optimum; the tell is that "optimal" sits over conflicting human-cost axes with no measurement that ranks them.
